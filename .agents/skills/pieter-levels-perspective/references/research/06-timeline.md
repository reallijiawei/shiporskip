# Pieter Levels (@levelsio) -- Life & Career Timeline

## Overview

Pieter Levels is a Dutch indie hacker, digital nomad, and serial solopreneur. He is the founder of Nomad List, Remote OK, PhotoAI, and numerous other projects. Known for bootstrapping all ventures solo with no VC funding, building primarily in PHP, and being radically transparent about revenue.

---

## Timeline

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **1987** | Born on December 14 in the Netherlands | Dutch nationality; father was a cardiologist |
| **~2005-2009** | Attended university in the Netherlands (studied business/industrial engineering) | Did not study computer science; later became a self-taught developer |
| **~2010-2012** | Early freelance web development work | Built foundational coding skills; experimented with small web projects |
| **2013** | Moved to Chiang Mai, Thailand to bootstrap startups | Embraced the digital nomad lifestyle; wrote about "income arbitrage" -- earning Western income while living in low-cost countries |
| **2014 (Jan)** | Launched **"12 Startups in 12 Months"** challenge | Radical "ship fast" philosophy that became hugely influential in the indie hacker movement |
| **2014** | Built **Play My Inbox** (startup #1) | Tool that collected music from email inboxes and played them in a visual player |
| **2014** | Built **Go Fucking Do It** (startup #2) | Goal accountability tool that charged your card if you missed deadlines; raised $30K+ in pledges |
| **2014** | Built **Tubelytics** (startup #3) | Real-time analytics dashboard SaaS for YouTube publishers |
| **2014** | Built **Nomad List** (startup #4) | Live index ranking cities for digital nomads by cost, internet, safety, weather. Hit #1 on Product Hunt and Hacker News. Became his flagship product |
| **2014** | Built **Nomad Jobs** (startup #5) | Job board for remote workers featuring distributed companies like Buffer, Zapier, Automattic |
| **2014** | Built **GifBook** (startup #6) | Service to print animated GIFs as physical flip books |
| **2014** | Built **#nomads** (startup #7) | Chat community for digital nomads; grew to 2,000+ members with worldwide meetups |
| **2014-2015** | Built **Remote | OK** (startup #8) | Daily aggregator of all remote jobs using sentiment analysis. Hit #1 on Product Hunt. Became his second major product |
| **2015** | Reported full-year revenue of **$202,785** | Achieved with "one Linode server, one MacBook, no office, no house" -- validated the lean solopreneur model |
| **2017** | RemoteOK generating **$2,342/day** | Described as "a single PHP file" generating significant daily revenue |
| **2018** | Published book ***MAKE: The Indie Maker Handbook*** | Self-published guide on bootstrapping startups; became a foundational text for the indie hacker community (sold at makebook.io) |
| **2018** | Won Product Hunt **"Maker of the Year"** award | Recognized for shipping the most successful products on the platform |
| **2018** | Reached **$52,843 in monthly revenue** | Steady growth across Nomad List and Remote OK portfolio |
| **2019** | Hit **$1M annual revenue** milestone | "It took 4 years" -- combined revenue from Nomad List and Remote OK |
| **2020** | RemoteOK reached **$65,651/month** | COVID-19 pandemic massively accelerated remote work adoption, boosting his core products |
| **2021** | Described running **"a $10M+ business"** | Portfolio-wide revenue growth; solidified position as one of the most successful indie hackers |
| **2022 (Oct)** | Launched **AvatarAI.me** (later PhotoAI) | Pivoted into AI-generated photos; made **$100,000 in 10 days** from AI-generated avatars. Marked his entry into the AI product space |
| **2022** | Launched **InteriorAI.com** | AI-powered interior design visualization tool; another AI-native product in his portfolio |
| **2023** | **PhotoAI.com** generating **$61,808/month** | Described as "almost 14,000 lines of raw PHP" -- proved complex AI products could be built with simple tech stacks |
| **2023** | Continued launching AI-adjacent products | Expanded portfolio with multiple AI-powered tools; became a vocal advocate for AI-first product development |
| **2024** | Hit **$420,000/month** revenue record | All-time high monthly revenue across his product portfolio |
| **2024** | Appeared on **Lex Fridman Podcast** | Major mainstream visibility; discussed indie hacking philosophy, digital nomad life, AI products, bootstrapping vs. VC |
| **2024** | Met **Patrick Collison** (Stripe) and **Sam Altman** (OpenAI) | Crossed into mainstream tech recognition beyond the indie hacker niche |
| **2025** | Reached **1 billion views on X (Twitter)** in 12 months | Became one of the most influential tech voices on the platform; massive audience growth |
| **2025** | Continued solo operation of full product portfolio | Maintained 100% ownership, no employees, 99% profit margins across all products |

## Core Philosophy (reflected across timeline)

- **No VC funding** -- 100% bootstrapped, 100% ownership
- **Ship fast** -- MVP-first, iterate based on traction
- **Solo founder** -- minimal or zero employees
- **Simple tech stack** -- PHP, jQuery, SQLite; rejects over-engineering
- **Build in public** -- radical revenue transparency on X/Twitter
- **Digital nomad lifestyle** -- location-independent since 2013
