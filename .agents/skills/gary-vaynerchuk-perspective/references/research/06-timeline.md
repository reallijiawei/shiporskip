# Gary Vaynerchuk — Life & Career Timeline

A chronological reference of key events, milestones, and ventures.

---

## Early Life & Immigration

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1975 | Born Gennady Alexandrovich Vaynerchuk on November 14 in Babruysk, Soviet Union (now Belarus) | Belarusian roots shape his immigrant work ethic narrative |
| 1978 | Immigrated to the United States at age 3 with family | Family settled in Queens, New York; later moved to Edison, New Jersey |
| ~1986 | Began running lemonade stands and trading baseball cards at local malls/flea markets | First entrepreneurial ventures; earned thousands of dollars as a child |
| ~1989 | Joined the family retail wine business (Shopper's Discount Liquors) at age 14 | Working in the store after school; began learning the wine and retail trade |
| 1998 | Graduated from Mount Ida College with a bachelor's degree in management science | Formal education complemented his hands-on business experience |

---

## Wine Library Era (1998–2011)

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1998 | Took over his father's liquor store (Shopper's Discount Liquors) in Springfield, NJ | Renamed it **Wine Library**; began aggressive modernization |
| 1999–2005 | Grew Wine Library from ~$3M to ~$60M in annual revenue | Leveraged e-commerce, email marketing, and early internet adoption — years ahead of competitors |
| 2006 (Feb) | Launched **Wine Library TV** (WLTV) on YouTube | One of the first daily web shows; 1,000+ episodes produced over 5 years; pioneered wine content for mainstream audiences |
| 2008 | Published *Gary Vaynerchuk's 101 Wines* | First book; wine-focused, cementing his authority in the space |
| 2009 | Published *Crush It!: Why NOW Is the Time to Cash In on Your Passion* | NYT Bestseller; became a manifesto for the personal branding and hustle movement |
| 2010 | Launched *Wine & Web* on Sirius XM satellite radio | Expanded into audio/radio; bridging wine culture with tech and entrepreneurship topics |
| 2011 (Aug) | Announced the end of Wine Library TV; launched **Daily Grape** (short-lived mobile wine show) | Stepped away from wine to focus entirely on VaynerMedia and digital marketing |

## VaynerMedia & VaynerX (2009–Present)

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 2009 | Co-founded **VaynerMedia** with brother AJ Vaynerchuk | Social-media-first digital agency; started small, leveraging Gary's personal brand |
| 2011 | Published *The Thank You Economy* | Second business book; argued that social media demands genuine customer care |
| 2013 | Published *Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook: How to Tell Your Story in a Noisy Social World* | Social media marketing strategy guide; "give value before you ask" framework |
| 2014 | Launched *The #AskGaryVee Show* on YouTube | Q&A format show answering fan questions on entrepreneurship, marketing, and self-awareness |
| 2015 | VaynerMedia named to **Ad Age's A-List** | Industry validation; agency had grown significantly with major brand clients |
| 2015 | Started **DailyVee** — daily vlog/documentary series on YouTube | "Document, don't create" philosophy in practice; behind-the-scenes of his entrepreneurial life |
| 2015 | Named to **Crain's New York Business 40 Under 40** | Recognition as a leading NYC entrepreneur |
| 2016 | Published *#AskGaryVee* (book) | NYT Bestseller; distilled show content into a leadership and social media guide |
| 2016 | VaynerMedia reached ~600 employees and ~$100M in revenue | Rapid scaling; major clients included PepsiCo, GE, Anheuser-Busch, Chase, Toyota |
| 2016 | VaynerMedia partnered with Vimeo for digital content | Expanding agency capabilities into original content production |
| 2017 | Formed **Gallery Media Group** (originally "The Gallery") | VaynerX subsidiary; acquired PureWow; launched ONE37pm.com — a lifestyle/culture digital publication |
| 2017 | Launched **VaynerSports** | Sports talent agency; focused on helping athletes build personal brands and off-field business |
| 2017 | Appeared on Apple's *Planet of the Apps* alongside will.i.am, Jessica Alba, Gwyneth Paltrow | Mainstream TV exposure; judged app pitches on the show |
| 2017 | Listed as one of **Forbes' Top Social Influencers** | Cemented status as a top-tier social media personality |
| 2018 | Published *Crushing It!: How Great Entrepreneurs Build Their Business and Influence* | Updated follow-up to *Crush It!*; NYT Bestseller |
| 2018 | Created **VaynerX** as the parent holding company | Restructured corporate umbrella: VaynerMedia, Gallery Media Group, VaynerTalent, VaynerSpeakers, VaynerCommerce, VaynerProduction |
| 2019 | Co-founded restaurant reservation app **Resy** (founded 2014 with Ben Leventhal and Michael Montero) — acquired by **American Express** | Successful exit; Resy integrated into AmEx's dining benefits ecosystem |

## Ventures, Acquisitions & New Frontiers (2019–Present)

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 2019 | Co-founded **Empathy Wines** (DTC wine brand) | Direct-to-consumer model; leveraged his massive social media following to disrupt traditional wine retail |
| 2020 (Jul) | **Constellation Brands** acquired Empathy Wines (~$50M deal) | Major exit; proved DTC brand-building via social media could scale to acquisition by a legacy beverage giant |
| 2021 | Published *Twelve and a Half: Leveraging the Emotional Ingredients Necessary for Business Success* | Focused on emotional intelligence in business; personal growth themes |
| 2021 (May) | Launched **VeeFriends** — NFT project on Ethereum | 10,255 hand-drawn character NFTs; each tokenized a human trait (e.g., Empathy, Accountability); many granted access to VeeCon |
| 2021 (Oct) | VeeFriends doodles sold for **$1.2M at Christie's auction** | Bridging NFT art with traditional auction houses; major cultural moment |
| 2022 | Held **VeeCon 2022** in Minneapolis | First major NFT-ticketed superconference; speakers from business, sports, and entertainment |
| 2023 | Held **VeeCon 2023** in Indianapolis | Continued community-building; emphasis on real-world utility over speculative NFT value |
| 2024 | Published *Day Trading Attention* and *Meet Me in the Middle* | Two new books; focus on attention economy and entrepreneurship |
| 2024–25 | Continued expansion of **VeeFriends** into toys, books, and entertainment IP | Evolved from NFT project into a broader consumer brand |
| 2025–26 | Remains Chairman of VaynerX, CEO of VaynerMedia; prolific daily content creator | Active across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, and podcast (*The GaryVee Audio Experience*) |

## Key Themes Across the Timeline

1. **Immigrant hustle narrative** — Arrived with nothing; work ethic as brand foundation
2. **Early internet adoption** — E-commerce for wine in 1998; YouTube in 2006; social media marketing before it was mainstream
3. **Content-first business building** — Personal brand as the flywheel for all ventures
4. **"Document, don't create"** — Transparent, high-volume content over polished production
5. **Platform arbitrage** — Consistently moved to emerging platforms (YouTube → podcast → TikTok → NFTs) before mass adoption
6. **Empathy and emotional intelligence** — Increasingly emphasized self-awareness, gratitude, and patience (especially in *Twelve and a Half*)
